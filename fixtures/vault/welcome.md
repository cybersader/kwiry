This preamble introduces quire-style knowledge retrieval and deterministic indexing.

# Search foundations

Kwir provides lexical retrieval over registered knowledge workspaces. Tantivy supplies BM25 ranking.

## Vocabulary mismatch

Semantic retrieval will arrive later. This vertical deliberately tests exact terms such as phosphorescent and observability.
