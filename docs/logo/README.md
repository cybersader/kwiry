# kwiry logo concepts

Three two-tone rounded-pixel magnifying-glass concepts. Pick one (or direct changes) and it becomes the repo/plugin icon.

## Rounded Pixel Search

![Rounded Pixel Search](rounded-pixel-search.svg)

- Palette: #182238 — deep ink for the lens rim and handle; #23D5C3 — vivid aqua for the lens interior and handle edge accent.
- A literal magnifying glass is assembled from rounded grid blocks, with an octagonal pixel-ring silhouette and a stepped diagonal handle that remain legible at 32×32. The vivid lens fill preserves the search shape on dark surfaces, while the deep ink outline and handle stay crisp on light surfaces.

## Pixel Note Lens

![Pixel Note Lens](pixel-note-lens.svg)

- Palette: #182338 — deep ink for the upper-left lens structure; #14B8A6 — vivid teal for the magnified text, lower-right lens structure, and handle.
- A rounded, block-built lens frames three compact text dashes, making the local knowledge-search idea immediate even at 32×32. The split-tone ring keeps the silhouette legible across light and dark surfaces, while overlapping handle blocks preserve a crisp pixel character when scaled to 256×256.

## Rounded Pixel Lens

![Rounded Pixel Lens](rounded-pixel-lens.svg)

- Palette: #0F172A — deep ink badge and lens negative space; #22D3EE — vivid cyan rim and magnifying-glass blocks.
- A deep rounded-square badge frames an electric-cyan magnifying glass assembled from softly rounded grid blocks. The stepped lens and handle remain unmistakable at 32×32, while the one-unit accent rim preserves the silhouette on both light and dark backgrounds.
